#vlsi #characterization #glossary
## glossary
short definitions of terms that come up across the IISc notes. the quantum ones at the bottom link into the quantum notes. part of [[Metrology and nano-characterization workshop]]
## characterization terms

| term | meaning |
|---|---|
| **adventitious carbon** | thin layer of carbon contamination on anything exposed to air, used as the C 1s = 284.8 eV reference in [[XPS]] |
| **ATR** | attenuated total reflectance, sample pressed on a crystal for [[FTIR]] |
| **Auger electron** | electron ejected when an outer electron fills a core vacancy, energy independent of the X-ray source ([[XPS]]) |
| **backscattering** | laser in and Raman light out along the same path through one objective ([[Raman spectroscopy]]) |
| **BSE / SE** | backscattered / secondary electrons: composition / topography contrast in the [[SEM]] |
| **Bragg's law** | $n\lambda=2d\sin\theta$ ([[XRD#^bragg]]) |
| **camera length** | the effective distance from sample to screen in a [[TEM]] diffraction pattern, $Rd=\lambda L$ |
| **contact potential difference (CPD)** | work function difference between tip and sample, measured by KPFM ([[AFM]], [[UPS]]) |
| **curtaining** | vertical streaks when [[FIB]] milling a non-uniform sample |
| **dipole moment** | charge separation, must change for IR absorption ([[FTIR]]) |
| **EAL / IMFP** | effective attenuation length / inelastic mean free path of electrons ([[XPS]]) |
| **EOT** | equivalent oxide thickness: the SiO₂ thickness with the same capacitance as a high-k film ([[MOS capacitor C-V]]) |
| **Fermi level** | energy where the occupation probability is ½ |
| **flat band voltage** | gate voltage where the bands in a MOS capacitor are flat, $V_{FB}$ ([[MOS capacitor C-V]]) |
| **fluorescence** | broad light emission that can swamp Raman peaks |
| **GIXRD** | grazing incidence XRD for thin films |
| **guard** | extra conductor in a triax cable held at the signal voltage to kill leakage ([[MOS capacitor C-V]]) |
| **HAADF** | high angle annular dark field: STEM detector whose brightness goes like $Z^2$ ([[TEM]]) |
| **interaction volume** | the teardrop-shaped region under an electron beam that the signals come from ([[SEM]]) |
| **interferogram** | detector signal vs mirror position in an FTIR, the Fourier transform gives the spectrum |
| **lamella** | thin slice (<100 nm) cut by [[FIB]] for [[TEM]] |
| **mean free path** | average distance between scattering events |
| **ohmic / Schottky contact** | linear vs rectifying metal–semiconductor contact ([[Sheet resistance and contacts]]) |
| **pass energy** | energy the XPS analyzer lets through, lower = better resolution, less signal |
| **phase imaging** | tapping-mode image of the drive/response delay, shows material contrast ([[AFM]]) |
| **polarizability** | how easily the electron cloud is distorted by an electric field ([[Raman spectroscopy]]) |
| **quality factor $Q$** | $f_0/\Delta f$: how sharp a resonance is, how long it rings ([[MSA]]) |
| **Rayleigh scattering** | elastic scattering, same colour as the laser |
| **reciprocal space** | Fourier space of a crystal, where diffraction spots live ([[XRD]]) |
| **R_sh** | sheet resistance, $\rho/t$, in Ω/sq |
| **SAED** | selected area electron diffraction ([[TEM]]) |
| **set point** | reference value (force or amplitude) the AFM feedback holds |
| **sputtering** | knocking atoms out of a surface with ions ([[FIB]], [[XPS#depth profiling]]) |
| **Stokes / anti-Stokes** | scattered photon has lost / gained a vibration's energy |
| **subthreshold swing** | mV of gate per decade of current below threshold, ≥60 mV/dec at 300 K ([[Electrical characterization]]) |
| **Tauc plot** | the straight-line plot that gives a bandgap from absorption ([[UV-vis spectroscopy]]) |
| **TLM** | transfer length method for contact resistance ([[Sheet resistance and contacts]]) |
| **transfer length** | distance over which most current crosses into a contact, $L_T$ |
| **UHV** | ultra high vacuum, ~$10^{-9}$ mbar or better |
| **van der Pauw** | sheet resistance of any shaped flat sample with four edge contacts |
| **work function** | energy to take an electron from the Fermi level to vacuum, $\phi$ ([[UPS]]) |

## the quantum terms

| term | meaning | where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **de Broglie wavelength** | every particle is a wave with $\lambda=\frac hp$ | [[SEM#^de-broglie]], [[Electron microscope]] |
| **photon** | one packet of light energy, $E=h\nu$ | [[XPS#the quantum connection]], [[Single Photon making and detecting]] |
| **quantized levels** | a bound particle can only have certain energies | [[Particle in a box]], [[EDS#the quantum connection]] |
| **tunnelling** | a particle getting through a barrier it classically couldn't cross | [[AFM#the quantum connection]], [[TEM#the quantum connection]] |
| **Pauli exclusion** | no two electrons in the same quantum state | [[UPS#the quantum connection]], [[AFM]] |
| **spin** | the electron's built-in two-level angular momentum, a natural qubit | [[XPS#the quantum connection]], [[Bloch sphere]] |
| **phonon** | one quantum of vibration | [[Raman spectroscopy#the quantum connection]], [[MSA]] |
| **plasmon** | one quantum of the electrons sloshing together | [[REELS and other XPS modes#the quantum connection]] |
| **quantum confinement** | squeezing a particle raises its energy levels ($\propto\frac1{a^2}$) | [[UV-vis spectroscopy#the quantum connection]] |
| **Josephson junction** | two superconductors with a ~1–2 nm barrier that pairs tunnel through, the heart of a superconducting qubit | [[TEM]], [[Sheet resistance and contacts#the quantum connection]] |
| **charging energy** | the energy to add one electron to a small capacitor, $\frac{e^2}{2C}$ | [[MOS capacitor C-V#the quantum connection]] |
| **resistance quantum** | $\frac h{e^2}=25.8$ kΩ | [[Sheet resistance and contacts#the quantum connection]] |
| **Fourier transform** | splits a signal into its frequencies, what diffraction does physically | [[XRD#the quantum connection]], [[Quantum Fourier Transform]] |
