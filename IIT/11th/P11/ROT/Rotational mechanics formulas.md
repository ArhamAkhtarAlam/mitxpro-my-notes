## quick results

| problem | answer |
|---|---|
| $m$ moves $d$ relative to a free $M$ | $M$ moves back $\frac{m}{m+M}d$ |
| shell splits at the top, one half drops straight down | other lands at $\frac{3R}{2}$ |
| one half retraces its path | other lands at $2R$ |
| flat car loaded at $\mu$ from rest | $v=\frac{Ft}{m_0+\mu t}$ |
| cart leaking at $\mu$ | $v=\frac F\mu\ln\frac{m_0}{m_0-\mu t}$ |
| free rod hit at an end, quarter turn | centre moves $\frac{\pi L}{12}$ |
| puck stops after hitting a stick at $d$ | $m=\frac{M}{1+12d^2/L^2}$ |
| solid hemisphere / cone | $\frac{3a}{8}$ / $\frac H4$ from the base |
