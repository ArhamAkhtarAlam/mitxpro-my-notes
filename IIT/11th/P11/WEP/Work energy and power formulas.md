## quick results

| problem | answer |
|---|---|
| block placed gently on a spring | max stretch $=2\times$ rest stretch |
| leaving a circular track (from $R$ above the centre) | $\sin\theta=\frac23$, max height $\frac{23}{27}R$ |
| chain, $\frac13$ hanging, friction $\mu$ | $K=\frac{2MgL}{9}(2-\mu)$ |
| equal masses, elastic | swap velocities |
| bullet $m$ into block $M$ on spring $k$ | $x=\frac{mv}{\sqrt{k(M+m)}}$ |
| projectile, average power of gravity | $0$ |
